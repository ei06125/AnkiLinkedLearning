# Transcript cards

Capture individual Japanese LinkedIn Learning lessons as they are visited. Keep lessons and cards locally. Select a kanji or highlight a word, retaining its complete surrounding sentence.

Card front: kanji and sentence. Card back: kanji, reading, sentence, translation, and lesson source. Export UTF-8 text for Anki Desktop without an add-on.

Readings and translations are currently entered manually. Automatic enrichment is undecided. Live extraction still needs verification against an authenticated LinkedIn Learning lesson; pasted transcripts provide a fallback.
